      How it works

      Levels

      Each set of tones is learned three ways: pick the number, type it, then tune a tone to it. The sets grow: 500, 1,000 and 2,000; the low octaves; the high octaves; all seven octaves; half-, third- and quarter-octaves.

      Then the piano, C3 to C8: pick the frequency, then the note, then the key. Last comes any frequency: typed within 6%, or tuned within 10%.

      A perfect 20 of 20 moves you up a step; 12 or fewer moves you back one.

      Scramble

      Fast bursts of pitches close to what you just heard, alternating with noise, four times over. It erases the last tone so you can't answer by comparison.

      Find the tone

      Spin the record until the tone sounds like the number; within 10% passes. Every target gets a new random set of pitch stops, so turns can't be counted, and past either end the tone fades out and comes back at the other end. The first Find steps use one tone at a time: 1,000, then 500, then 2,000, then all three.

      Memory

      Pull is how far your answers lean toward the tone before them. Cold is the first answer of a round after 10 quiet minutes.
